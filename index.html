Commands

Everything
LootSnag can do.

/freegames
Lists all games currently free to claim on Epic, Steam, and other tracked stores.
/deals
Fetches today's best active deals across all tracked stores as rich embeds.
/wishlist add [game]
Add a game to your tracker. Get notified the instant it drops in price or goes free.
/wishlist remove [game]
Remove a game from your wishlist. Alerts for it stop immediately.
/wishlist list
View your full wishlist with current prices and any active deals.
/currency [INR / USD]
Set your preferred currency. All prices convert automatically.
/settings
Configure alert channels, deal thresholds, and enabled stores for your server.
